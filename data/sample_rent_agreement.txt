RENT AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is made at Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh on this 1st day of April 2026 between:

Shri Ramesh Chandra Agrawal, S/o Late Shri Kailash Narayan Agrawal, aged about 58 years,
resident of E-7/44, Arera Colony, Bhopal (M.P.) 462016, hereinafter called the LESSOR;

AND

Ms. Anjali Verma, D/o Shri Suresh Verma, aged about 26 years, presently employed at Bhopal,
hereinafter called the LESSEE.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 302, Second Floor, Shanti Residency,
Kolar Road, Bhopal (M.P.), consisting of two bedrooms, a hall and a kitchen (the "Premises").

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

1. TERM. The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing from 1st April 2026,
and shall stand automatically renewed for successive terms of eleven months each unless the
Lessor gives contrary intimation, so that the Lessee may continue in occupation for up to
thirty-six months in all.

2. RENT. The Lessee shall pay rent of Rs. 50,000 per month for the Premises, payable in
advance on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month, by bank transfer only.

3. ESCALATION. The rent shall stand enhanced by 12% on completion of every eleven months.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Lessee has paid to the Lessor an interest-free security deposit of
Rs. 3,00,000 (Rupees Three Lakh only), being six months' rent, which shall be refunded within
ninety days of vacation after deduction of such sums as the Lessor may in his sole discretion
determine.

5. LOCK-IN. There shall be a lock-in period of twenty-four months. The Lessee shall not vacate
the Premises during the lock-in period, and the Lessor is under no corresponding restriction.

6. PENALTY. In the event the Lessee vacates the Premises before the expiry of the lock-in
period, or commits breach of any term hereof, the Lessee shall pay to the Lessor a penalty of
Rs. 50,000 in addition to forfeiture of the entire security deposit, whether or not the Lessor
proves any loss.

7. UTILITIES. Electricity and water charges shall be borne by the Lessee as per actual meter
readings. If rent remains unpaid for fifteen days, the Lessor shall be entitled to disconnect
the electricity and water supply to the Premises without notice until dues are cleared.

8. NOTICE. The Lessor may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving seven days' notice,
whereas the Lessee shall give three months' notice in writing.

9. ENTRY. The Lessor or his agents shall be entitled to enter the Premises at any hour for
inspection, without prior intimation to the Lessee.

10. USE. The Premises shall be used for residential purposes only. No subletting is permitted.

11. REPAIRS. All repairs, whether structural or otherwise, shall be carried out by the Lessee
at her own cost, and the Lessor shall bear no expense whatsoever.

12. STAMP. The Agreement is executed on stamp paper of Rs. 100 and both parties agree that no
further formality is required.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the day, month and year first above
written.

LESSOR: Ramesh Chandra Agrawal          LESSEE: Anjali Verma
